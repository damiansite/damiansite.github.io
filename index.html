Camps and Courses

Model Aircraft and Drones Course

2018 — Organised by Fablab and Tekwill

Learned the basics of aerodynamics for a glider. Constructed a glider and tested it for resistance and flight duration, achieving 2nd place. The final project involved controlling a motorised glider and keeping it airborne for as long as possible.

Third Edition of the Summer School "The Path to Scientific Discoveries"

June 2025 — Organised by the Academy of Sciences of Moldova

Selected participant at a summer school program bringing together scientists, scholars, and experts from diverse scientific disciplines. Received theoretical and practical instruction in scientific development, research methodology, and the relationship between science and society through lectures and discussions. The program deepened my interest in the field of research.

Second Balkan Student Summer School on Quantum Physics

August 2025 — Organised by the Balkan Physical Union, European Physical Society, Hellenic Physical Society, and others

Participated in an international summer school on the fundamentals of quantum physics and its applications, bringing together students from across the Balkan region. Gained an understanding of wave-particle duality, superposition, and entanglement, and their relevance to modern quantum technologies such as quantum computing and quantum sensing. Attended lectures and laboratory sessions reproducing classic experiments, including the photoelectric effect, the double-slit experiment, and the Franck–Hertz experiment.
